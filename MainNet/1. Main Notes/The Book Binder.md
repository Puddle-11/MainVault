# The Book Binder

Genre:

Main Concept:
    Play as a humble book binder in a small occult town in England. Witches and Warlocks will come to you with loose pages, and its your job to take them and bind them into a tome. Use spells and magic to quell the violent spells and bind them safely to satisfy the customer. Collect money and grow your business. (think [[Stardew Valley]] level progression)
Player Hook:
    Witchy book binding. Witch aesthetic with the mundane task of book binding.
Game Vision:
    Quaint village with cute graphics like [[Stardew Valley]] or [[Animal Crossing]]. Artistic direction like [[Crimson Hollow]]

Tone:
    Light hearted and cozy
Core Loop:
    Bind books to get money to expand to bind harder more challenging books and expand.
Gameplay Overview:
    Each book will have a slightly different process. The most basic process which is introduced is the book binding itself, minigame details can be sorted later but it should be relatively detailed, i.e taking the leaflets and sewing them together, then selecting and sewing the leather onto the page block.
    Sometimes the magic in the pages will need to be neutralized or pacified before binding, or sometimes you will need special leather to bind a book. A lot of variations can be achieved through just a few different mini game options. With lets say a maximum of 3 steps in a book binding process, with 7 minigames you have 35ish different combinations, and each of those can have slight variations as well, with specific randomized ingredients needed for each step.
Player Experience:
    Cozy interactive minigames with book binding.
