# Temporary Effects
---
### Dizziness 
While dizzy, the screen will spin slightly and the players walking direction will shift back and forth slightly.
### Exhaustion  
While exhausted, all fervor is useless, and the player must find a bed quickly, or they will fall asleep where they stand, defenseless. 
### Fatigued
While fatigued, movement is slowed and attacks do less damage. 
### Insane
While insane, the player can see the dead, aswell as interact with them. 
### Envigored
While Envigored, fervor will not deplete. Upon the effect ending, the player will receive the fatigued attribute.
