# Hand Of god

Genre:
    City or Automation Builder
Main Concept:
    You are a deity in a post apocalyptic world. (i want this world to be based off of zdzislaw beksinski's work). You must build up a brutalist city or factory to produce what you need (tbd). Could be fear, or devotion. I think fear would be better or maybe life essence cause i have this image of a line of people walking into a factory that you set up so you know exactly what is about to happen to them, that image is just very provocative.

    play as an evil or malignant god.
