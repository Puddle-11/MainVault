# Business Sim

Genre:
    Comedy, business sim
Main Concept:
    You're a corporate CEO and you eat your employees soul to raise your stock prices, newer talent is worth less, senior talent is worth more. Losing to much newer talent can scare senior talent away. Being sued, investigations, the news, etc are all aspects that can affect your stock.
Player Hook:
    Kill your employees to get profit

Core Loop:
    Keep your stocks up by continually hiring and killing your employees. Drop to low and your clients company will file for bankruptcy. More importantly you will lose the soul in the contract.

    Manage stocks, public appearance, employee retention, and many more to keep your shareholders happy.

    Lure your employees into back rooms away from prying eyes, to preform the sacrifice.
### Game Goals

Primary Goals:
