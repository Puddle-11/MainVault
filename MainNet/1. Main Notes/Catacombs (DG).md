# Catacombs (DG)
---
## Description
The catacombs are one of the very first areas in the game, its on the easier side, and its first half is used to introduce mechanics to the player. 
## Layout
The level design of the catacombs consists of almost entirely stone brick walls with torches scattered about, Skeletal remains used as set dressing. For specific generation details, see vermis catacombs
