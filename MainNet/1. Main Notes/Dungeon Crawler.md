# World Lore
## Things in flux
the gods could be also old kings of the world who has master control over their domains
the realm names
the god names


This world has been overrun by not just evil but fundamental disorder. The pantheon of gods that kept this world in balance has disappeared, and with it, all the fundamental rules of this world. Each god controlled a difference domain, and unlike domains in our world, each of these domains existed parallel to one another, meaning each world existed ontop of one anther, only being able to be traveled to under specific means. Despite their discrete nature, each domain did effect the others. 
These domains, consist of the following. 
[[The Song (DG)]]
[[The Divine (DG)]]
[[The Heresy (DG)]]
[[The Apostate (DG)]]
In this world each God sustains a realm. 
[[Nara The God of Song]]
[[Lerina The God of Divinity]]
[[Thissel The God of Heresy]]
[[Selkra The God of Apostasy]]



when the gods left the realms started to collide. Worlds that should never have directly interacted, now the barriers were open. After thousands of years of this, the worlds have decayed and new power regimes have formed, to take place of what was left behind. 
As the gods interacted with the world they imbued some of their power into objects and entities. When entities spend to long in contact with the dreamers, they become [[Enlightened]], although this process takes hundreds of years, When someone becomes [[Enlightened]], They gain a fundamental understanding of the world and as a result most go mad (represented by local realm bosses in game) Those who havnt gone mad, fight for power in this new world. [[Enlightened]] entities bend the world around them, shaping it to their will. Those who have gone mad behave like wild animals, fighting for territory with others, only the strongest survive. Before the gods left, they were able to stabilize those [[Enlightened]] beings, and keep them from going mad. 
After the fall, when every realm collided, Those who were enlightened already, started reshaping the new world to their will. Those who weren't enlightened, became victims, losing their humanity.

On top of all the personal effects that have been a cause of the fall, many other natural rules no longer are in place. Before the fall, the souls of those that died, left this realm, however now they are left to wander. Inhabiting and infecting anything they can get their hands on. This leads to many new enemy types being created due to the fall.
