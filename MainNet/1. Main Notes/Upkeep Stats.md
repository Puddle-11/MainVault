# Upkeep Stats
---
### Health 
How much health the player has before dying
Reaching zero will result in death

### Hunger
How hungry the player is
Hunger will decrease strength. 
Upon reaching zero, health will start to decay, Hunger alone can not kill the player
If hunger reaches zero, the player gains the fatigued attribute. 

### Thirst
How thirsty the player is
Thirst decreases speed.
Upon reaching zero, health will start to decay, Thirst alone can not kill the player
If thirst reaches zero, the player gains the dizzy attribute. 
### Sanity
How sane the player is
Upon reaching zero Fervor will begin to deplete
As sanity decreases, the player will see more hallucinations. Hallucinations can include audio queues, enemies seen in the real world, false items or world events, and in fights, false attacks.  
If sanity reaches zero, the player gains the insane attribute. 

### Fervor
How much will the player has left
Fervor is used to preform certain attacks or tasks in the world. 
If fervor reaches zero, the player will gain the Exhausted attribute.
