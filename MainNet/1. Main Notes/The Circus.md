# The Circus

Genre:
	Horror
Main Concept:
	You are a child visiting a circus when you get separated from your parents, and find many horrifying things below the surface of this circus.
Player Hook:
    Unreliable narrator. I don't want the true horror of this game to be supernatural, but instead a foreign place through the eyes of a child. From the beginning. the starting scene should be you with your family, and everything looks and feels normal and happy, but when you are separated from your parents things slowly start getting more scarry. I also want real horrifying things really happening in the circus, but i want the player to always question if its real or the child's imagination.
Game Vision:
	Something like little nightmares where the player is navigating these scenes of people distorted

Tone:
Dark
Core Loop:
    Each room will be an area in the circus that you need to get through, think little nightmares or inside. The player will progress through these levels one after another and avoid the staff and audience to make it out.
Gameplay Overview:
    No real game mechanics system beyond movement and interaction with scene objects. I think it would be cool to give the character a polaroid camera.
    Potential Idea | Polaroid Camera:
    Maybe the polaroid camera can be used in certain circumstances to ward off guests or staff from attacking or "getting" you. OR the camera can just be used to take photos of the world, Then a final scene of the game can be looking back at the photos with your family and seeing they are all normal except for a couple, further pushing that idea of what was really going on in that circus.
    Potential Idea | Polaroid Photo:
    polaroid photo of their family before they got separated. Maybe Include some sanity system where as the player panics they kind of shut down, and curl up into a ball, the enemies and architecture can get more imposing until the player looks at the polaroid. This is just an idea
