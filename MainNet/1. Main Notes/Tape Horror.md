# Tape Horror

Genre:
Horror Mystery
Main Concept:
You work in a strange office that takes in tapes (cassette and vhs) of strange occurrences. Each time you "examine" a tape, a gameplay segment starts that consists of you living out the occurrences in the tapes. 
Player Hook:
unlike normal horror, each experience is different and each one lasts between 10 and 20 minutes
Game Vision:

Unique Selling Point:
Self contained micro-horror with many easy off ramps. semi-non linear.
Target Audience:
streamers or horror fans, age demographic 16-30

Tone:
Dark
Core Loop:
Insert tape, play mini-experience, repeat
Gameplay Overview:

Player Experience:
Most of the gameplay segments will be pretty linear. A lot of the gameplay comes from discovering or finding out what happened and maybe some gameplay elements in the "real world" in the office.
### Game Goals

Primary Goals:
Figure out the larger conspiracy, or escape from the facility
Secondary Goals:
Complete the most amount of tapes possible.
If the player dies in the tape, jump back to the room where the "character" is "watching" the tapes, and show the tape malfunctioning and getting destroyed. 
Moment to Moment Goals:
Survive in the tapes.
### World and Level Design

Game World Overview:
the game world will consist of one central room, with piles of unorganized tapes, and many mini-levels of self contained horror experiences.
