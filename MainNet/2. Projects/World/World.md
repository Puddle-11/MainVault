The world is a merging of 4 realities. Each one sustained by a sleeper, With the reality being the dream of that entity. The entities are 
[[Prayer]]
[[Song]]
[[Worship]]
[[Divine]]

Each dream is represented by a symbol, displayed in the world circle.
An [[Awakened]] entity is an entity that exists in all dreams simultaneously. Entities that are not [[Awakened]] Only exist in one dream, and can use [[Focus]] To effect their [[Dream]], other [[Dream]]'s, or move between [[Dream]]'s.
